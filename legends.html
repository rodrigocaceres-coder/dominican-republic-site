Dominican Sports Hall of Fame

There is a hall of fame at home too: the Dominican Sports Hall of Fame (Pabellón de la Fama del Deporte Dominicano) in Santo Domingo. Since 1966 it has honored athletes and sports promoters each year, and baseball always has many inductees.
