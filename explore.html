Before you go

Quick travel tips

  1. Carry some pesos in cash for colmados, buses and small markets.
  2. Pack sunscreen and insect repellent, especially at sunset.
  3. Learn a few Spanish phrases. A greeting opens many doors.
  4. Drink bottled water and eat at busy food stands.
  5. Book nature excursions with authorized local guides.
